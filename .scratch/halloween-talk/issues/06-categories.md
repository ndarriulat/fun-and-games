# 06: Halloween Categories

**Status:** done

The phone shows a category. Each round can be played two ways: Bidding (bids out loud, top bidder enters their bid, then 60 seconds to name them; the group marks made it / missed) or Writing (60 seconds for everyone on paper or notes, then the group says who had the longest list). About 40 categories, each with 10–30 possible answers, in both languages.

No player names: the phone keeps the category, timer and round only. Saved on the phone; a refresh mid-timer comes back paused.
