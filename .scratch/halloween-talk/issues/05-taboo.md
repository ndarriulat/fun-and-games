# 05: Halloween Taboo

**Status:** done

Two teams, one phone. Each team plays 2 turns of 60 seconds, alternating. The phone shows a word and its forbidden words; Correct +1, Buzz (said a forbidden word) −1, Skip up to 3 times a turn. Between turns: "Team 2, pass the phone". At the end: scores and the winner. About 50 words per language, written separately for each.

Saved on the phone after every tap; a refresh mid-turn comes back paused with the time left.
