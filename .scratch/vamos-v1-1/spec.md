# Spec: Vamos v1.1 (Puzzles)

v1 ships Trivia and Talk. Puzzles was moved out of v1 (decided 2026-10-03) because it's the least important mode for the group, and comes back in v1.1.

v1.1 adds the Puzzles mode and its 100 items (25 per type: Riddles, Logic, Maths, Word games) in both languages, then a short road test.

The design is unchanged and lives in the v1 spec (`.scratch/vamos-v1/spec.md`): the Puzzles user stories (2, 20, 23, 35–40), the Puzzle item shape, the Puzzles interaction details, the content check rules for Puzzles and the Puzzles content targets. Puzzles plugs into the finished v1 app: Filters, language, theme, Report, history and the design from v1 all apply.
